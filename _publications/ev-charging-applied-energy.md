**Status:** Manuscript in preparation for *Applied Energy*; data-algorithm-claim alignment in progress<br>
**Context:** Collaboration with an EV charging operator serving European customers

This work calibrates each vehicle's natural AC charging envelope and schedules mixed single- and three-phase charging sessions under aggregate and per-phase grid constraints.

The primary operational metric is **service stretch**: actual residence time relative to the vehicle's standalone charging time. The scheduling problem seeks work-conserving, grid-compliant allocations that reduce congestion-induced stretch while respecting vehicle charging curves and phase-specific capacity.

The current research gate is substantive rather than editorial. Parts of the available data and the present algorithmic argument do not yet support the same claim boundary. Before submission, the model, data coverage, comparator set, and algorithmic claims are being reconciled; unsupported claims remain outside the manuscript.
